Diabetes is a chronic disease that occurs when the pancreas does not produce enough insulin or when the body cannot effectively use the insulin it produces. This leads to high blood glucose levels.

Common symptoms of diabetes include frequent urination, increased thirst, unexplained weight loss, fatigue, and blurred vision.

Hypertension, also known as high blood pressure, is a condition where the force of blood against artery walls is consistently too high. It often has no symptoms but increases risk of heart disease and stroke.

Anemia is a condition in which the blood lacks enough healthy red blood cells to carry adequate oxygen to body tissues. Common symptoms include fatigue, weakness, and pale skin.
